# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24

This policy covers the Chrome extensions **Form Autofill**, **Auto Refresh & Page Watch** and **Tab Vault**, published by Aslan ("we"). [Türkçe](gizlilik-politikasi)

## In short

- The extensions work **only on your device**. They never send your data to us or to anyone else: there are no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no remotely loaded code.
- The data each extension needs is stored in your browser's extension storage and stays there until you delete it or uninstall the extension.
- We never sell, share or transfer your data, and we never receive it in the first place.

## What each extension handles, and where

| Extension | What it stores (only in your browser) | When it reads web pages |
|---|---|---|
| Form Autofill | The rules you create: field names or patterns, the values to fill (which may include personal details you choose to save, such as your name, email address or postal address) and the sites they apply to; your settings. | Only when you click Fill or Save this form (or use the shortcut or right-click menu), or when a page loads on a site for which you turned on automatic filling. Password fields are never read or filled; card numbers are never saved. |
| Auto Refresh & Page Watch | Your timers (the tab's address and title, the interval, the text you watch for) and your settings. | Only on the tabs where you started watching, after each reload, to check for the text you asked for. The page text is compared on your device and is not stored. |
| Tab Vault | The addresses and titles of the tabs you save, the groups you create, the trash and automatic copies made before each update; your settings. Tabs in private (incognito) windows are never saved. | Only when you save tabs, to read their address and title. |

## Network

The extensions make no network requests. Each extension's content security policy blocks its pages and background script from connecting to any server, and this is verified by automated tests before every release. Site icons in Tab Vault come from Chrome's own icon cache.

If a future version adds paid features that need to verify a license key with a payment provider, this policy will be updated before that version is published, and only the license key will be sent for that purpose.

## Permissions

Each extension asks only for the permissions it needs. Access to a website is requested only for the specific site you choose, at the moment you need it, and can be removed at any time. The permissions and the reasons for them are listed on each extension's Chrome Web Store page.

## Your control

- You can view, edit, export and delete your data inside each extension.
- Uninstalling an extension deletes all of its data from your browser. Backup files you downloaded stay in your Downloads folder until you delete them.

## Children

The extensions are general-purpose tools and do not knowingly handle data from children.

## Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

The use of information handled by these extensions adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
